Before the word, there was the spark. Not metaphor — actual light, the first photon escaping the density of everything that had not yet become anything.

She had read about it in a book whose title she could no longer remember. That seemed appropriate. Origins have a way of erasing their own traces.

---

The fire in the hearth was the oldest technology. Older than language, older than agriculture, older than the distinction between self and other. To sit before a fire was to participate in the first act of civilization.

She watched the flames and thought about beginnings. How every one of them was also a forgetting.n
