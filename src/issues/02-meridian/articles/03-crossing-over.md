at the meridian the compass needle
trembles between two norths —

                  not lost
                  but liberated
                  from the tyranny
                  of singular direction.



i have stood on lines
that do not exist
and felt them
                  hold me.

every border is a bridge
seen from the wrong altitude.

every wall is a door
painted shut
        by someone
        who forgot
        they held the key.



                  cross over.
the other side
is also
        here.
